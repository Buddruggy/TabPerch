# Privacy policy

TabPerch is a free, open-source Chrome extension published by Buddruggy.

## Data handled on your device

To group tabs, close idle tabs, and let you reopen closed pages, TabPerch reads and stores:

- tab URL, title, and domain
- when a tab was last accessed, and when it was closed
- why it was closed
- your settings, including idle time, allowlist, and whether automatic closing and sorting are on

This data is stored in `chrome.storage.local` in your browser profile. Incognito windows are ignored.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. TabPerch has no account, no server, and no analytics. It does not sell, share, or transmit tab data or settings to the developer or any third party.

## How long it is kept

Saved URLs stay until you delete them in the popup or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes the extension's local storage. Settings stay until you change them or uninstall.
